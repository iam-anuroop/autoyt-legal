Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Autoyt and its Meta app Typhon Publisher are a personal tool operated by its owner, Anuroop T, only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account. The app has no user sign-ups and does not collect personal data from other users.

1. Ask for deletion by email

If you want any data related to you deleted, send an email to autoytigfb@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please say which account or data the request is about. Your request will be handled, and you will receive a reply, within 30 days.

2. Remove the app's access on Facebook

You can also remove the app's access to your account at any time:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites (on some accounts this is called Business integrations).
  3. Find Typhon Publisher and select Remove.

On Instagram, the same option is under Settings → Apps and websites. Once access is removed, the app can no longer reach your account.
