Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

In short: Autoyt is a personal tool used only by its owner to run the owner's own Typhon pages (YouTube channel, Instagram @aut.oig, Facebook Page Typhon). It publishes the owner's own explainer videos and emotional posts and does nothing else. No user data is collected, and no data from any other person is collected, sold or shared.

1. Who uses Autoyt

Autoyt ("the tool") is a personal automation tool operated by its owner for the owner's own use. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts or sign-ups, and is never used with anyone else's Google, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram account. It runs the owner's Typhon pages only.

2. Data the tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube: only the permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). The tool does not request access to the owner's Gmail, contacts, Drive, viewing history, comments, analytics or any other Google data.
  • Meta (Facebook Page and Instagram): only the permissions needed to publish the owner's own explainer videos (Reels), image posts and short music videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens that these permissions produce.

3. How the data is used

The permissions and tokens are used solely to upload and publish the owner's own explainer videos, image posts and short videos to the owner's own channel, Page and account, and to check that an upload finished. They are not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, training AI or machine-learning models, or any other purpose.

4. Photos, music and other services

  • Photos: free, licensed stock photos are found through the Pexels and Pixabay APIs using generic search words (for example "rain on window"). No personal data is sent. The tool keeps only the photo's source, id, photographer name and page link (to credit the photographer and never reuse a photo); the image itself is not stored.
  • Music: tracks the owner downloaded manually under free licences (Pixabay Content License, CC0, CC BY). Credits are added to captions when a licence asks for attribution.
  • Optional AI (Google Gemini API): may be used to write short original quotes and to judge the stock photos. It receives only theme words and stock photo previews, never personal data.
  • Notifications: the owner receives status messages about their own posts through a private Telegram bot.

5. Sharing and selling

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party. No data from other users is collected at all. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video upload goes to YouTube).

6. Storage and security

  • Tokens are stored encrypted: in a private database that only the owner controls (Fernet, AES encryption), and in the hosting provider's encrypted secret settings. They are never published, logged or committed to source code.
  • Rendered images and videos exist only in a temporary folder and are deleted as soon as they are published (and in any case within 6 hours). No media is kept in the database or on the server.
  • The database keeps only small text records of past posts (explainer topic; or theme, quote, photo reference and credits), so that no topic, photo or quote is ever repeated, plus publishing logs that are purged after 90 days.
  • Access to the tool's server and database is restricted to the owner.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the tool and are deleted when it is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites)
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites

After revocation the tool can no longer access the account, and the stored tokens stop working.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Autoyt's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the upload feature described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except where needed for security purposes or to comply with applicable law.

9. Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
