Autoyt

A personal automation tool for publishing short videos.

Autoyt is used only by its owner. It uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not a public service: there are no user sign-ups, and no other person's account or data is ever accessed.

What it does

  • Creates short vertical videos from the owner's own content.
  • Uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, using permissions the owner granted to the tool.
  • Nothing else: it does not read, analyse or collect anyone's personal data.
