Autoyt

The personal automation tool behind the Typhon pages.
Emotion, Logic and Science – the impossible combination.

Autoyt is used only by its owner to run the owner's own Typhon pages: the Typhon YouTube channel, the Instagram account @aut.oig and the Facebook Page Typhon. It is not a public service: there are no user sign-ups, and no other person's account or data is ever accessed.

What it does

  • Explainer videos (Logic and Science): short hand-drawn videos in which Inku, a little stick man, explains an everyday thing in about 30 seconds. Every fact is checked and its sources are listed. Published as a YouTube Short, an Instagram Reel and a Facebook Reel.
  • Emotional posts (Emotion): one calm photo with one short original quote, as an image post, or as a short video of the same image with licensed background music. Published to Instagram and the Facebook Page only (never to YouTube).
  • Publishes only the owner's own content to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page, using permissions the owner granted to the tool.
  • Photos come from free, licensed stock libraries (Pexels, Pixabay) and are credited in every caption. Music comes from tracks the owner downloaded under free licences (Pixabay Content License, CC0, CC BY). The explainer narration is generated on the owner's own computer.
  • Nothing else: it does not read, analyse or collect anyone's personal data.
